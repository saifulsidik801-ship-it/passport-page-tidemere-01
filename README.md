An invented passport spread for the Republic of Tidemere — a tidal archipelago whose only land link is a causeway passable at low water. The data page belongs to Elena Maris Voss, a licensed tide pilot of Port Lowick; the visa page carries her 2024 travel history (Aldervik, Kessig), a tide table, a chart of the isles, and a ferry levy stamp. Every stamp falls after the passport's issue date of 02 FEB 2024 and inside its validity, the Aldervik visa covers the March trip exactly, and the machine-readable zone carries valid ICAO check digits.
